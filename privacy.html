Privacy

Your information in Outcome1

What Outcome1 stores, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to take it with you or delete it.

Last updated 17 November 2026

What is stored

  • Your sign-in. The email address and password you sign up with. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to Outcome1.
  • Your training profile. What you enter during setup, such as your name, training number (NTN or GMC number), email, specialty, region and training dates.
  • Your records. Requirement counts and ticks, logbook entries (clinics, procedures, CPD, simulation, teaching, acute takes and ward cover), your ARCP preparation, notes you write and your CCT calculator answers.
  • Files you upload. CPD certificates you choose to attach, up to 10 MB each.
  • Teaching feedback. When you share a feedback link, attendees’ answers are stored with that teaching session. The form doesn’t ask for their names and doesn’t need a sign-in. It shows them your name and the session details you entered, such as the topic and date.

Patient information

Outcome1 is for your own training records. Please don’t enter anything that could identify a patient, in notes, logbook entries or uploaded files.

Where it is kept

Your records are stored with Google Firebase: sign-in by Firebase Authentication, records in a Firebase Realtime Database located in Belgium (europe-west1), and uploaded certificates in Firebase Cloud Storage.

A copy of your records is also kept in your browser on each device you use, so the app opens quickly and works without signal.

The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records basic technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts, and tools for spreadsheets, images and QR codes from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These services see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Who can see it

Your records are stored under your own account and are meant to be seen by you when you are signed in. Teaching feedback answers are shown only to the person who shared the link.

The person who runs Outcome1 can technically reach the database to keep the service working, but does not look at individual accounts unless you ask for help with a problem.

Your information is never sold or shared with anyone else. Outcome1 has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s storage only to keep you signed in, keep your copy of your records and remember your settings.

Taking it with you, or deleting it

  • Download a copy. In the app, go to Account, then Your data, then Back up. You get one file with everything in your account.
  • Delete your account. Account, then Your data, then Delete my account. This removes your records, your uploaded certificates, your teaching feedback and your sign-in.
  • How long it is kept. Until you delete your account. The copy on a device stays until you sign out or clear the site’s data in your browser.

Questions

For anything about your information, including a request to see or delete it, email contact@outcome1.uk.
