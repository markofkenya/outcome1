About Outcome1

Your ARCP portfolio, in one place

Outcome1 is a free portfolio tracker for UK physician trainees. It keeps your ePortfolio requirements, your training logbook and your ARCP preparation together, so you can see what is done and what is left before your panel.

Open Outcome1

What it does

  • Tracks your requirements. Yearly and end-of-training targets for your specialty and for GIM, such as SLEs, MCRs, MSF raters, clinics and CPD hours.
  • Keeps a logbook. Clinics, procedures and DOPS, CPD, simulation, acute takes and ward cover, counted towards your targets as you log them.
  • Gets you ready for ARCP. A list of what is due before your portfolio locks, a Firth calculator, a logbook spreadsheet, specialty and GIM summaries, a CPD diary and a CCT date calculator.
  • Collects teaching feedback. Share a link or QR code and attendees fill in the teaching evaluation form anonymously, without signing in.
  • Works on your phone. It opens without signal and keeps your records in step across your devices.

What it is not

Outcome1 does not connect to your ePortfolio or to any NHS system. You enter your own numbers, and you still upload your evidence to your ePortfolio yourself.

It is for your own training records only. Please don’t enter anything that could identify a patient.

Who runs it

Outcome1 is an independent project, built and run by a UK specialty registrar. It is free to use during the beta, with no adverts and no tracking.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHS, the JRCPTB, the Royal Colleges or any deanery.
